How to read the scores and features

Pointwise ID describes the geometry around an excerpt’s representation relative to a shared reference set. It is not an intrinsic score of a text’s linguistic complexity. The explorer shows measured estimates, rather than predictions from the random forest.

Percentiles use every available aligned estimate within the selected model, layer, and estimator. They are calculated before any search or filter. Raw ID scales differ across settings; a percentile locates an excerpt within its own distribution.

Features come from Profiling-UD and three recorded frequency controls. Word counts use UD words. Percentages have the denominators stated in each feature’s definition. Lexical density is displayed as a percentage of its stored proportion.

Coverage includes 1,994 excerpts with aligned profiles from the 2,000-excerpt evaluation set. A missing FCI estimate is shown as unavailable. The three frequency controls are shared Pythia-tokenizer proxies in the released data. Excerpts retain the terms of their original Pile sources.
